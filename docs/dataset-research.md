# Dataset Research

## HAM10000

Number of Images:
10,015

Number of Classes:
7

Source:
Harvard Dataverse / ISIC Archive

Notes:
- One of the most widely used skin lesion datasets.
- Contains dermatoscopic images.
- Includes melanoma and benign lesions.
- Good starting dataset for training skin cancer classification models.

## Fitzpatrick17k

Number of Images:
16,577

Importance for Darker Skin Tones:
- Contains skin type annotations based on the Fitzpatrick scale.
- Includes more diverse skin tones than many traditional dermatology datasets.
- Useful for evaluating fairness and performance across different skin tones.

Source:
Stanford-led research dataset

Notes:
- Valuable because darker skin tones are often underrepresented in medical AI datasets.

## ISIC Archive

What is it:
International Skin Imaging Collaboration Archive

Approximate Size:
Tens of thousands of skin lesion images and growing.

Source:
ISIC

Notes:
- Largest public repository for skin lesion images.
- Hosts HAM10000 and challenge datasets.
- Standard benchmark used in skin cancer AI research.
